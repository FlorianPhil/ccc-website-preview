Tools and tips.

Seven ways cannabis networking events pay off, and four steps for getting more from the next one.

View events Read the steps

01Why attend

Seven benefits of going to a cannabis networking event.

If you work in cannabis, whether you run a business, provide services or want to enter the market, showing up matters. The Chamber hosts about 75 events a year, from local mixers to international symposiums and members-only global calls.

  1. 01

    Meet decision-makers face to face

    The fastest way to open doors is a real conversation. Events bring together brand founders, retailers, manufacturers, investors and regulators, the people who can introduce you to distribution, partnerships or capital. A well-timed introduction at a mixer can replace weeks of cold outreach.

  2. 02

    Get market intelligence in real time

    Panels, keynote talks and expert roundtables show you what is changing now, from regulatory updates to product trends. Symposiums and curated panels give you context that headlines alone do not, and help you spot opportunities before they are mainstream.

  3. 03

    Learn practical skills and compliance best practices

    Many gatherings include workshops, job fairs and training sessions. They are a low-cost way to learn compliance, supply-chain strategy or marketing technique from people who have already solved the problems you face.

  4. 04

    Build brand credibility and visibility

    A consistent presence at regional events and industry panels builds trust. Speaking, sponsoring or simply taking part shows that your business is serious, which makes potential partners and customers more comfortable working with you.

  5. 05

    Expand both local and global reach

    Smart event programs blend the local and the global. Local mixers help you find nearby partners and retail relationships. Virtual global calls and international symposiums extend your network to new markets and investors without a long trip.

  6. 06

    Recruit and assess talent in person

    Hiring at events lets you meet potential hires in a way a resume cannot show. You can judge fit, drive and cultural alignment quickly, and shorten the time to hire for specialized roles.

  7. 07

    A good return on your time and budget

    Compared with broad advertising or endless cold outreach, targeted events often produce higher-value leads faster. Small mixers and curated panels can lead to warm introductions that turn into customers or partners with far less friction.

02Before you go

Four steps for a better event.

  1. 1Go with an objective. Set one or two goals, such as finding a distribution partner or recruiting a compliance manager.
  2. 2Prepare a 30-second pitch and two clear asks.
  3. 3Bring quality business cards or digital contact options.
  4. 4Follow up within 48 hours with a brief, personalized note.

Networking is a strategy. The right mix of local mixers, educational panels and global calls turns conversations into contracts and insight into action.

To find the right opportunities for your goals, check the events calendar for regional mixers, international symposiums and members-only global calls.
