About this event

IgniteIt Spotlight: California, Official After-Party

Hosted by The Cannabis Chamber of Commerce, in partnership with Emerald Village and Ian Rassman

The conversations don't have to end when the day's programming wraps. Join us for the official After-Party following IgniteIt's Market Spotlight: California. Plan for a relaxed evening to reconnect with the people you met earlier in the day, meet a few new faces, and keep building the relationships that move your business forward.

Set in the warm, plant-filled patio of The Woods WeHo in West Hollywood, this is cannabis networking without the pitch-deck energy: good company, good drinks, and the kind of conversations that actually lead somewhere.

When: October 7, 2026 · 7:00 to 9:00 PM

Where: The Woods WeHo, 8271 Santa Monica Blvd, West Hollywood, CA

Admission: IgniteIt speakers and sponsors on the event gate list will receive complimentary admission.

For all other guests, The Woods requires either a $20 minimum purchase or a $10 cover charge at the door.
