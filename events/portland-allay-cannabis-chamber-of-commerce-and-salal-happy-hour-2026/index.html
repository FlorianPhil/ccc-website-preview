About this event

Overview

Allay Consulting and Cannabis Chamber of Commerce are combining forces! Please join us with co-host Salal Credit Union!

Please join us to network and meet new folks in this awesome industry we all work in!

Allay Consulting:

Allay Consulting is a plant medicine supporting compliance consulting firm with locations in Denver CO and Portland OR. We specialize in cGMP/GACP/Organic/ISO certifications along with FDA, OSHA, and state specific complaince. Allay Consulting is comprised of former cannabis regulators that now support plant medicine companies with their compliance goals. Please visit www.allayconsulting.com to see all of our services.

Cannabis Chamber of Commerce:

The Cannabis Chamber of Commerce is the leading global network for cannabis industry professionals, businesses, and advocates. With roots in the United States and expansion across the globe, we are building an international community that connects the people shaping the future of legal cannabis, across continents, cultures, and industries.

Salal Credit Union:

Salal Credit Union has been in business since 1948. At a time when women couldn’t get an account or credit card by themselves, 4 of our original 8 founding members were women. This speaks to our progressive nature since inception. That progressive nature lead us to be one of the first financial institutions to bank the cannabis industry more than a decade ago. We offer those in the cannabis industry the same business banking products and services that every other industry in the country is able to access, including a suite of lending products. In addition to assisting the business, we also roll out the red carpet for business owners and their employees. In the complex and ever-changing world of cannabis banking, experience really does matter. Since 2014 Salal Credit Union has been a national leader in providing financial services to the cannabis industry, its owners, and employees. Looking to turn your cannabis business vision into a reality? Lets talk.

We would love for you to join us for drinks and networking with some of the best companies in the biz. See you there!
