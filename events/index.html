04Event FAQ

Questions about attending and hosting events.

Other event questions go to the Chamber team by email.

Contact the Chamber

Do members get access to exclusive events and workshops?

Yes. One of the most valuable perks of Cannabis Chamber membership is exclusive access to events, workshops, and networking mixers. Members receive invitations to both in-person and virtual gatherings, including educational seminars, job fairs, and business development forums. Many of these events are free or discounted for members, providing significant value while expanding opportunities for collaboration. Workshops cover essential topics such as compliance, marketing, investment, and global cannabis trends. In addition, regional chapters host local networking events that bring together professionals in specific markets. By participating, members gain knowledge, visibility, and industry connections that are essential for success.

What types of events does The Cannabis Chamber host?

The Cannabis Chamber organizes a wide variety of in-person and virtual events designed to foster networking, education, and professional growth. These include:

  • Networking mixers where members connect with peers and potential partners
  • Educational workshops and seminars covering compliance, marketing, and investment strategies
  • Virtual forums for global members who can’t attend in person
  • Cannabis job fairs connecting employers with qualified professionals
  • Industry conferences and chapter meetings focused on local markets

Events are scheduled monthly across different regions, ensuring that members can consistently engage with the community. Many are free or discounted for members, offering high value while helping cannabis businesses gain visibility and new opportunities.

Are there virtual events for members outside of the U.S.?

Yes. The Cannabis Chamber regularly hosts virtual events and workshops to ensure that international members receive the same benefits as those based in the U.S. These events include educational webinars, networking sessions, and industry forums designed to connect professionals across borders. Members from Canada, Europe, Latin America, and beyond can participate without travel, making it easier to stay informed and engaged with the global cannabis industry. Virtual events cover a range of topics, from compliance and policy updates to investment trends and market expansion strategies. For global professionals, these sessions provide a convenient way to connect with industry leaders, exchange knowledge, and build relationships.

How often does The Cannabis Chamber hold events?

The Cannabis Chamber hosts events on a monthly basis, with activities taking place across different regions and online. Local chapters organize in-person mixers, networking events, and educational workshops, while the global Chamber schedules virtual sessions to connect members internationally. Larger gatherings, such as job fairs and regional conferences, are typically held quarterly or annually depending on demand. Members are notified in advance through email newsletters and event calendars, ensuring they can plan ahead. This consistent schedule provides members with regular opportunities to build connections, gain insights, and promote their businesses throughout the year.

Can non-members attend Cannabis Chamber events?

In many cases, yes. While most Cannabis Chamber events are exclusive to members, non-members are often welcome to attend selected mixers, educational forums, and networking sessions. This allows potential members to experience the value of the Chamber before joining. However, members always receive priority access, discounts, and exclusive invitations to premium events. Non-member attendance is typically subject to a fee, which can be credited toward membership if they decide to join. By participating, non-members gain exposure to the Chamber’s professional network and can better understand how membership will benefit them long-term.

Where can I find the Cannabis Chamber events calendar?

The Cannabis Chamber’s official events calendar is available on our website under the Events page. This calendar is updated regularly and includes details about upcoming networking mixers, virtual workshops, job fairs, and regional chapter meetings. Each listing provides information about event topics, dates, locations, and registration requirements. Members receive direct invitations via email, but the public events calendar ensures transparency and easy planning. The calendar also highlights which events are exclusive to members and which are open to non-members. By checking regularly, you can stay informed and take full advantage of the networking and educational opportunities offered throughout the year.

Are there local chapter networking events in my city?

The Cannabis Chamber operates through a combination of global initiatives and local chapters, ensuring members have opportunities to connect both online and in person. Local chapters host networking events, mixers, and educational sessions tailored to the needs of their region. These events are designed to foster community building, collaboration, and local business development. Members can check the Events Calendar on our website to see upcoming gatherings in their city or nearby areas. If a chapter doesn’t yet exist in your region, members may also inquire about starting a local chapter with support from the Chamber. This structure ensures that cannabis professionals across the U.S., Canada, and international markets have access to meaningful networking opportunities in their own communities.

How do I partner with The Cannabis Chamber for an event?

Businesses or organizations interested in event partnerships or sponsorships can reach out through our Contact form or email us at Info@TheCannabisChamber.com. Partnerships may include co-hosting mixers, sponsoring workshops, or providing resources for educational sessions.
