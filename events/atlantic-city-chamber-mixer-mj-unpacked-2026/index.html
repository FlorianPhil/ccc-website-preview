About this event

We’re excited to host a Chamber Mixer during MJ Unpacked in Atlantic City.

Join The Cannabis Chamber at MJ Unpacked for an evening mixer sponsored by Magazzu Law Firm bringing together top operators, brands, and professionals across the cannabis industry. Connect with a curated audience in a relaxed setting designed for real conversations, meaningful relationships, and opportunities that extend beyond the conference floor.

MJ Unpacked is known for bringing together a highly curated group of operators, brands, and decision-makers, and this mixer is an opportunity to connect with that audience in a more relaxed setting.

If you’re attending, join us for an evening of conversation, connection, and community.
