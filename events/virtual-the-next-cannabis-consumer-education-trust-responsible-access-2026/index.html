About this event

As the regulated cannabis market evolves, a new wave of consumers will arrive with more questions, more caution, and a greater need for trusted guidance.

Retailers and brands are often the first place consumers bring questions about product types, consumption methods, cannabinoid profiles, dosage, safety, and medical use. But most canna businesses are not designed or staffed to provide individualized healthcare guidance.

This Cannabis Chamber of Commerce conversation brings together medical, educational, retail, and industry perspectives to map ways that businesses can prepare for an education-driven marketplace.

We’ll cover what first-time and medically curious consumers need, where the responsibilities of retailers and healthcare professionals differ, and how physicians, telehealth platforms, educators, brands, and operators can work together without turning dispensary staff into medical providers.

We’ll outline education as a business strategy: how better information can reduce consumer uncertainty, strengthen trust, improve the customer experience, and help companies differentiate (without relying only on price or potency).

Hosted live through Zoom & LinkedIn Live, with time for audience Q&A with a recording available through the Cannabis Chamber website and YouTube at a later date.
