Meet the Chamber's board of directors.

21 directors, many of whom also chair a regional chapter, bring experience from cannabis operations, consulting, law, finance, marketing, policy and investment.

01Expertise

Leadership drawn from across the industry.

The board and the regional leadership teams include experienced operators, industry experts, seasoned consultants and ancillary business leaders.

Experienced operators
Directors with hands-on experience running cannabis businesses.
Industry experts
Specialists with deep knowledge of the cannabis industry.
Seasoned consultants
Advisers who help cannabis businesses plan and grow.
Ancillary business leaders
Leaders of the service and support firms around cannabis operators.

Fields of experience

  • Law
  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Operations
  • Policy
  • Investment
