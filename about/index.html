01Mission

Why the Chamber exists.

The Chamber was formed to promote the common interests of cannabis industry professionals and to create new business opportunities for their brands.

02Footprint

Where the Chamber operates.

New chapters continue to launch as the Chamber expands its global footprint.

The Chamber has roots in California and has grown across the United States, Canada, Latin America, Europe, Asia, Australia and Africa. A member in New York and a member in the Netherlands share the same mission.

Each chapter is led by professionals who live and work in that market. Members get the relevance of a local chamber and the reach of a global organization.

03Regional model

How every chapter works.

Every chapter combines three parts.

  1. 01

    Local leadership

    A dedicated team in each region understands local regulations, business culture and growth opportunities, and creates introductions at the ground level.

  2. 02

    Global access

    Members reach the entire network beyond their own region. That opens doors to international partnerships, cross-border investment and expansion.

  3. 03

    Local market events

    Events, educational forums, job fairs and industry training are hosted by leaders who live and work in those markets.

The result is the relevance of a local chamber together with the reach of a global organization, from California dispensaries to European operators to Latin American businesses.

04Values

Five values guide the Chamber.

They shape how the team works with members, partners and the wider industry.

Integrity
Honesty, ethical practice and environmental responsibility in how the Chamber works with its team, its business partners and its industry connections.
Leadership
The time to lead is now. Members inspire each other to achieve and grow through a shared vision and passion.
Service
The Chamber serves its members, its communities and the regulated cannabis industry.
Community
A developing industry is a chance to build a better one together, with attention to sustainability, justice and recognition for the accomplishments of its members.
Compassion
The Chamber stands with its community and supports the people in it.

05Founder letter

A letter from the founder.

Chris Boudreau, Founder & Chair of the Board of Directors.

The Cannabis Chamber of Commerce is a global network for cannabis industry professionals, businesses, and advocates. Our organization was formed to promote the common interests of industry professionals by creating the potential to strengthen your brand and foster new business opportunities. With roots in the United States and expansion across the globe, we are building an international community that connects the people shaping the future of legal cannabis across continents, cultures, and industries.

From cultivation to commerce, policy to payments, we unite professionals who believe in the power of collaboration, education, and responsible growth. Whether you’re operating in New York or the Netherlands, our mission remains the same: To connect the cannabis industry with purpose, professionalism, and people.

Our local regional leadership teams help facilitate introductions and opportunities for members by bringing connections, products, and resources together. We do this with our regional networking events, educational forums, job fairs, industry training, and other chamber resources.

The goal is to grow together for a greater opportunity for us all. We hope you will join our efforts and become a member to help us accomplish something greater for everyone in the developing cannabis ecosystem.

Sincerely,

Chris BoudreauFounder & Chair of the Board of Directors
