03Upgrade later

What a paid tier adds.

You can upgrade anytime. Each paid tier includes everything in the tier before it.

  • Individual

    $20 per month or $200 per year

    Adds Business Directory access, an invitation to the Global Insights & Networking Call and free tickets to regional and monthly global events.

  • Silver

    $100 per month or $1,000 per year

    Adds one free event sponsorship booth in your region, the Global Leadership Directory, Silver logo placement, and local government advocacy and policy insight.

  • Gold

    $250 per month or $2,500 per year

    Adds a free booth at all regional events, 50% off booths in any region, the Global Membership Directory, Gold logo placement and an annual newsletter feature.

  • Platinum

    $500 per month or $5,000 per year

    Adds free booths worldwide, featured social and email announcements, a monthly newsletter feature, access to all contact lists and Platinum placement.
