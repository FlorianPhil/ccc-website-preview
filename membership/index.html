02Who each tier is for

Choose the tier that fits how you use the network.

  • Free

    $0

    Start here to build a networking profile, join the global community and use the private community forum.

  • Individual

    $20 per month or $200 per year

    For one professional who wants the Business Directory, an invitation to the Global Insights & Networking Call and free tickets to regional and monthly global events.

  • Silver

    $100 per month or $1,000 per year

    For a company that wants a free event sponsorship booth in its region, a place in the Global Leadership Directory and Silver logo placement.

  • Gold

    $250 per month or $2,500 per year

    For a company that exhibits across regions: a free booth at all regional events, 50% off booths in any region and an annual newsletter feature.

  • Platinum

    $500 per month or $5,000 per year

    For a company with a presence in many markets: free booths worldwide, monthly newsletter features, featured social and email announcements and access to all contact lists.

04Questions

Answers before you join.

Does the free networking profile cost anything?

No. The free networking profile costs $0. It includes a networking profile, global community access, the member discount directory and the private community forum.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Start with the free networking profile and upgrade anytime. Each paid tier includes everything in the tier before it, plus its own benefits.

What is the difference between monthly and annual billing?

Monthly billing charges the price shown for the tier each month. Annual billing charges once a year, at ten times the monthly price. Silver, for example, is $100 per month or $1,000 per year.

Can I join from outside the United States?

The Chamber has 40+ chapters across the United States, Canada, Latin America, Europe, Asia, Australia and Africa, and its online private network connects members around the globe. The join form asks for your country and region.

Where do I pay?

Payment is handled on a secure payment page after you submit the join form. The form itself never asks for card details.

Which events are free for members?

Many events are free or discounted for members. Individual membership and above include free tickets to regional and monthly global events. The events calendar shows what is coming up.

Other membership questions go to the Chamber team by email.
