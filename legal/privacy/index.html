Privacy Policy

The Association respects the privacy of its members, prospective members, website visitors, donors, event attendees, and others who interact with the Association. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we share it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, title, organization, email address, mailing address, phone number, payment information, and membership or event registration details. We may also collect information automatically when you use our website, such as device information, browser type, pages viewed, IP address, and cookies or similar technologies.

How We Use Information

We use information to administer memberships, process payments, communicate with members, provide benefits and services, register attendees for events, send updates and alerts, improve our website, comply with legal obligations, and maintain organizational records. We may also use your information to send Association communications, including renewal reminders, policy updates, and member notices.

How We Share Information

We may share personal information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website, process payments, send email communications, host events, or provide member services. We do not sell personal information in the ordinary course of our operations unless we clearly disclose otherwise and applicable law requires additional notice or choice.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site, improve performance, and enhance the user experience. You can usually adjust your browser settings to limit cookies, but some website features may not function properly if you do.

Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information. We retain personal information as long as needed to provide services, maintain membership records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

Your Choices

You may opt out of non-essential email communications by using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly. If applicable laws provide additional privacy rights to you, we will honor those rights as required.

Children

Our website and membership services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where required by law.
