01The network

Meet the buyers, partners and operators who move cannabis business.

The Cannabis Chamber of Commerce connects the people who run legal cannabis businesses: operators, consultants, investors and the service firms around them. Its mission is to connect the cannabis industry with purpose, professionalism and people.

The Chamber started in California. Today it runs 26 chapters across the United States, Canada, Latin America, Europe, Asia, Australia and Africa, and hosts about 75 events a year. Your local chapter is led by people who work in your market, so the introductions are real.

03How it works

Get deals, introductions and partners in your market, then in the world.

  1. 01

    People who know your market

    Each region is led by a team that knows its regulations, business culture and growth opportunities. They make introductions where business happens.

  2. 02

    Partners in every region

    Members reach the whole network beyond their own region, for international partnerships, cross-border investment and expansion.

  3. 03

    A room of people who do what you do

    Mixers, educational forums, job fairs and industry training, run by leaders who live and work in each market.
